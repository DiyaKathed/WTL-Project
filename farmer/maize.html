Maize Cultivation

Maize is an important cereal crop cultivated for food, feed and industrial purposes.

Season: Kharif and other suitable growing seasons depending on region and variety.
Soil: Well-drained fertile soil is generally preferred.
Water: Irrigation should be managed according to soil, weather and crop stage.
