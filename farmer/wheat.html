Wheat Cultivation

Wheat is an important cereal crop generally grown during the cooler growing season.

Season: Commonly grown as a Rabi crop in many parts of India.
Soil: Well-drained fertile soils are generally suitable.
Water: Irrigation should be scheduled according to crop stage and local conditions.
