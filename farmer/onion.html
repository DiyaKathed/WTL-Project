Onion Cultivation

Onion is an important vegetable and commercial crop cultivated in several regions.

Season: Growing season varies according to region and crop cycle.
Soil: Loose, fertile and well-drained soil is generally preferred.
Water: Regular and controlled irrigation is important, especially during bulb development.
